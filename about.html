About FixAnything

Practical help for everyday problems.

FixAnything is a free library of step-by-step guides covering the kind of problems that slow your day down — a stuck zipper, a stained shirt, a dripping tap, a gummed-up garbage disposal. The stuff you'd normally search for, wade through five blog posts to find, and still not get a clear answer.

Every guide is written to be direct and practical: what you need, what to do, what to watch out for. No filler, no paywalls, no sign-ups.

What we cover

  • Home — plumbing, cleaning, repair, electrical, and pets
  • Kitchen — appliances, cookware, and pests
  • Clothing — laundry, shoes, and repairs
  • Personal Care — everyday grooming and health fixes

Always free

All guides on FixAnything are and will always be free. The site is supported by unobtrusive advertising.
